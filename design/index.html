The idea

Three beliefs.

Everything on this page — and everything we build from it — traces back to one of these.

One —

Clarity is the luxury.

Fewer elements, more air. Nothing earns its place by default; if a visitor can't tell what's most important in three seconds, something gets cut.

Two —

Color is the joy.

Bright, committed fields of color — architecture, not garnish. When a page earns a moment of delight, it goes all in, then returns to calm.

Three —

One voice, many energies.

Serif speaks, sans works. The personal site turns the energy up; utility apps keep it crisp. Same tokens throughout — and it never follows the season.

Color

Four fields.
That's the whole palette.

Large, confident, and few. Each color is a field you can build on — not a chip you squint at.

Air
whitespace
The fourth color. Generous, deliberate, everywhere.
  • Color builds the page — full bands and big fields, not timid chips in corners.
  • One field at a time. Blue band or sunny band per viewport — never both competing.
  • No gradients, no muddy tones. Flat, bright, committed. Season-proof by construction.
100% fun

The fun has a home.

This band is the demonstration: when something deserves delight — a launch, a celebration, a feature worth shouting about — give it a whole field of sunny yellow and mean it.

"Nothing here yet — which is just room for something great."

Empty states, success messages, and small victories get the warm, lightly witty voice. Error messages stay plain and helpful. Fun never confuses.

Imagery

Lead with the visual.

Our references don't decorate with images — they open with them. One strong visual moment per page beats a gallery of small ones.

A big, bright visual moment lives here —
full-bleed band or large rounded panel.
  • Go big or skip it. Full-bleed bands or large 20px-radius panels — never small floats lost in the text column.
  • Bright, natural light. Photography should feel like morning — real moments over stock polish.
  • Illustration stays flat and bold — simple shapes in blue, sunny, and ink. The fuzzy blob is the mascot.
  • Every image earns its place with alt text and a reason to exist.
The rules

The gut-check.

When in doubt, run the design past this list.

Do

  • Fewer elements, more air
  • Color as architecture — full bands
  • Serif huge, sans quiet
  • Blue for actions, sunny for joy
  • Commit to the fun moment, then return to calm
  • Season-proof, always

Don't

  • Timid chips where a band belongs
  • Gradients, shadows-as-texture, muddy tones
  • Serif for body copy or buttons
  • Sunny yellow as text
  • Two color fields competing in one viewport
  • Design for a season
For the builder

How I apply this.

My checklist before anything ships:

  1. Start from the four fields — blue, sunny, ink, air. If a color isn't one of these, it doesn't go in.
  2. Compose in big moves — giant headline, full band, strong visual. No page should feel assembled from small parts.
  3. One color field per viewport. Blue or sunny — pick, commit, move on.
  4. Stay season-proof. If it feels like fall, it's wrong.
  5. Ship the draft for Rob's critique before anything goes live. Every time.
